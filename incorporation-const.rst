====================
Part 1 — Preliminary
====================

1. Definitions
--------------

(1) In this constitution:

   **committee member** means an office-bearer or ordinary committee member.

   **exercise a function** includes perform a duty.

   **function** includes a power, authority or duty.

   **office-bearer** means a committee member who is elected to an office referred to in clause 17(1).

   **ordinary committee member** means a committee member who is not an office-bearer.

   **register of members** means the register of members maintained under clause 7.

   **secretary** of the association means the person holding office under this constitution as Administrative Officer, or if no person holds that office, the public officer of the association.

   **special general meeting** of the association means a general meeting of the association other than an annual general meeting.

   **subcommittee** means a subcommittee established under clause 28.

   **the Act** means the Associations Incorporation Act 2009.

   **the Regulation** means the Associations Incorporation Regulation 2022.

(2) The Interpretation Act 1987 applies to this constitution as if it were an instrument made under the Act.

2. Name
-------

The official name of the association is **Software Development Society Inc.**

3. Objects
----------

The objects of the association are:

   a. To create a welcoming and active community for discussion of software development;
   b. To engage in creating, developing and maintaining software projects;
   c. To support education for individuals interested in software development;
   d. To facilitate culture and team relations within the software development community;
   e. To encourage the software development culture at UNSW;
   f. To facilitate opportunities for open-source development.

4. Guide for Meetings
---------------------

In all matters not specifically dealt with herein, the procedures set out in the latest edition of Guide for Meetings and Organisations by N.E. Renton shall apply.

Part 2 — Members of Association
===============================

5. Membership Generally
-----------------------

(1) An individual is taken to be a member of the association if:

   a. the person applied to be a member under clause 6(1) and the application has been approved, or
   b. the person was one of the individuals on whose behalf an application for registration of the association was made under the Act, section 6(1)(a), or
   c. the person was a member of the unincorporated body immediately before incorporation.

(2) A person who is not an individual is not eligible to be a member of the association.

(3) Full membership of the association shall be open to all UNSW students.

(4) Associate membership shall be open to all persons who are not UNSW students.

(5) The duration of a person's membership shall be until the association's next Annual General Meeting after they have become a member.

(6) The association shall comply with Anti-Discrimination legislation in all of its activities and procedures, including the granting of membership.

6. Membership Applications
--------------------------

(1) An application by a person to be a member of the association must be:

   a. made in writing or by electronic means as determined by the committee, and
   b. in the form determined by the committee, and
   c. lodged with the Administrative Officer.

(2) The Administrative Officer must refer an application to the committee as soon as practicable after receiving the application.

(3) The committee must approve or reject the application.

(4) As soon as practicable after the committee has decided the application, the Administrative Officer must give the applicant written notice of the decision, including by email or other electronic means.

(5) The Administrative Officer must enter the applicant's name in the register of members as soon as practicable after the applicant pays any required fees.

(6) The applicant becomes a member once the applicant's name is entered in the register.

7. Register of Members
----------------------

(1) The Administrative Officer must establish and maintain a register of members of the association.

(2) The register:

   a. may be in written or electronic form, and
   b. must include, for each member:
   
      i. the member's full name,
      ii. a residential, postal or email address,
      iii. the date on which the person became a member,
      iv. if the person ceases to be a member, the date on which the person ceased to be a member, and
   
   c. must be kept in New South Wales at the association's main premises or official address, and
   d. must be available for inspection, free of charge, by members at a reasonable time.

(3) Contact details for members are to remain with the Committee and shall not be given or sold to any other person, except as required by law.

(4) Information about a member, other than the member's name, must not be made available for inspection if the member requests that the information not be made available.

(5) A member must not use information about a member obtained from the register to contact or send material to the member, unless the information is used to send material relating to the association or it is necessary to comply with a requirement of the Act or the Regulation.

8. Fees and Subscriptions
-------------------------

(1) Members shall be required to pay an annual membership fee as set by the committee.

(2) The entrance fee and annual subscription fee shall be determined by the committee from time to time.

9. Members' Liabilities
-----------------------

The liability of a member of the association to contribute to the payment of the debts and liabilities of the association or the costs of winding up is limited to the amount of any outstanding fees.

10. Disciplinary Action Against Members
---------------------------------------

(1) A person may make a complaint to the committee that a member of the association has:

   a. failed to comply with a provision of this constitution, or
   b. willfully acted in a way prejudicial to the interests of the association, or
   c. acted in a way that has sabotaged the functions of the association or disregarded the Constitution to the detriment of the association's membership, or
   d. instigated instances of bullying, harassment, assault and/or gendered violence to one or multiple individuals.

(2) The committee may refuse to deal with a complaint if the committee considers the complaint is trivial or vexatious.

(3) If the committee decides to deal with the complaint, the committee must:

   a. serve notice of the complaint on the member, and
   b. give the member at least 14 days from the day the notice is served within which to make submissions to the committee about the complaint, and
   c. consider any submissions made by the member.

(4) The committee may, by resolution, expel the member from the association or suspend the member's membership if, after considering the complaint, the committee is satisfied that:

   a. the facts alleged in the complaint have been proved, and
   b. the expulsion or suspension is warranted.

(5) If the committee expels or suspends the member, the Administrative Officer must, within 7 days of that action, give the member written notice of:

   a. the action taken, and
   b. the reasons given by the committee for taking the action, and
   c. the member's right of appeal under clause 11.

(6) The expulsion or suspension does not take effect until the later of:

   a. the day the period within which the member is entitled to exercise the right of appeal expires, or
   b. if the member exercises the right of appeal, the day the association confirms the resolution under clause 11.

11. Right of Appeal Against Disciplinary Action
-----------------------------------------------

(1) A member may appeal against a resolution of the committee under clause 10 by lodging a notice of appeal with the Administrative Officer within 7 days of being served notice of the resolution.

(2) The member may include, with the notice of appeal, a statement of the grounds for the appeal.

(3) The Administrative Officer must notify the committee that a notice of appeal has been received.

(4) If notified, the committee must call a general meeting of the association to be held within 28 days of the day the notice was received.

(5) At the general meeting:

   a. no business other than the question of the appeal is to be transacted, and
   b. the member must be given an opportunity to state the member's case, and
   c. the committee must be given the opportunity to state the committee's case, and
   d. the members present must vote by secret ballot on whether the resolution should be confirmed or revoked.

(6) The appeal is to be determined by a simple majority of votes cast by the members.

(7) Any member who believes they have been wrongly expelled may appeal to Arc Clubs Tribunal, who will arrive at the final resolution of the matter. Appeals must be submitted in writing within seven (7) days of receiving the penalty.

12. Resolution of Internal Disputes
-----------------------------------

(1) The following disputes must be referred to a Community Justice Centre within the meaning of the Community Justice Centres Act 1983 for mediation:

   a. a dispute between two or more members of the association in their capacity as members, or
   b. a dispute between one or more members and the association.

(2) If the dispute is not resolved by mediation within 3 months of being referred to the Community Justice Centre, the dispute must be referred to arbitration.

(3) The Commercial Arbitration Act 2010 applies to a dispute referred to arbitration.

13. Membership Entitlements Not Transferable
--------------------------------------------

A right, privilege or obligation that a person has because the person is a member of the association:

   a. cannot be transferred to another person, and
   b. terminates once the person ceases to be a member of the association.

14. Member Resignation
----------------------

(1) A member of the association may resign by giving the Administrative Officer written notice of at least 1 month of the member's intention to resign.

(2) The member ceases to be a member on the expiration of the notice period.

15. Cessation of Membership
---------------------------

A person ceases to be a member of the association if the person:

   a. dies, or
   b. resigns from being a member, or
   c. is expelled from the association, or
   d. fails to pay the annual subscription fee within 3 months of the due date.

Part 3 — Committee
==================

Division 1 — Constitution
-------------------------

16. Functions of Committee
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Subject to the Act, the Regulation, this constitution and any resolution passed by the association in general meeting, the committee:

   a. is to control and manage the affairs of the association, and
   b. may exercise all the functions that may be exercised by the association, other than functions required to be exercised in general meeting, and
   c. is responsible for:
   
      i. the activities of the association,
      ii. the finances of the association,
      iii. appointing members to subcommittees,
      iv. the maintenance and review of policies and procedures of the association, including its Grievance Resolution Policy and Procedure.

   d. The committee is at all times bound by the decisions of an Annual or Extraordinary General Meeting.

17. Composition of Committee
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) The committee must have 8 members, as elected in accordance with clause 18, consisting of the following office-bearers:

   a. A President (Non-Male Identifying);
   b. A President (Non-Female Identifying);
   c. An Administrative Officer;
   d. A Vice-President (Internals) & Welfare Officer;
   e. A Vice-President (Project Operations);
   f. A Vice-President (Externals);
   g. Two Vice-Presidents (Projects).

(2) One member is not permitted to hold more than one committee position.

(3) Job sharing of any committee position is not permitted.

18. Election of Committee Members
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) Any full member of the association may be nominated as a candidate for election to the committee.

(2) The nomination must be:

   a. made in writing or by electronic means, and
   b. entered and seconded by two full members, one of whom must be the nominee, and
   c. accompanied by the written consent of the candidate to the nomination, and
   d. given to the Returning Officer at least 7 days before the date fixed for the annual general meeting at which the election is to take place.

(3) Nominations for multiple positions must be ordered by preference.

(4) If insufficient nominations are received to fill all vacancies:

   a. the candidates nominated are taken to be elected, and
   b. a call for further nominations must be made at the meeting.

(5) If the number of nominations received is equal to the number of vacancies to be filled, the members nominated are taken to be elected.

(6) If the number of nominations received is more than the number of vacancies to be filled, a ballot must be held using an optional preferential voting system.

(7) If there is a tie for any committee position between candidates, the outgoing presidents shall have a casting vote. If they cannot agree, the entire executive will vote to decide.

(8) Only full members are entitled to vote for the committee.

19. Terms of Office
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) A committee member holds office from 1st December in the year in which they are elected until 30th November in the year following, or until the position is next duly filled.

(2) A member is eligible, if otherwise qualified, for re-election.

20. Vacancies in Office
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) A casual vacancy in the office of a committee member arises if the member:

   a. dies, or
   b. ceases to be a member of the association, or
   c. ceases to be a UNSW student (for positions requiring student status), or
   d. resigns from office by written notice given to the Administrative Officer, or
   e. has their position declared vacant at an Extraordinary General Meeting, or
   f. is absent from 3 consecutive meetings of the committee without apology or leave, or
   g. becomes an insolvent under administration, or
   h. is prohibited from being a director of a company under the Corporations Act 2001, or
   i. is convicted of an offence involving fraud or dishonesty for which the maximum penalty is imprisonment for at least 3 months, or
   j. becomes a mentally incapacitated person.

(2) The association in general meeting may, by resolution, remove a committee member from office and appoint another member to hold office for the balance of the term.

(3) A committee member to whom a proposed resolution relates may give a written statement to the Presidents or Administrative Officer and request that a copy be sent to each member at least 7 days before the meeting.

(4) Any vacancy on the committee must be filled at an Extraordinary General Meeting.

(5) Committee positions that become vacant less than 1 month before the yearly affiliation period may be filled by majority vote of the committee. People appointed this way will be 'Acting' in the position and may not be a President or bank signatory.

21. Returning Officer
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) At least one Returning Officer must be appointed by the committee prior to a General Meeting at which an election will take place.

(2) The Returning Officer must:

   a. ensure they are at all times impartial and objective,
   b. ensure that all elections are run fairly and in line with this constitution,
   c. prepare and circulate all notices of election, nominations, voting and proxies,
   d. provide all members with access to an email address designated for the Returning Officer,
   e. accept all nominations submitted that satisfy the rules of this constitution,
   f. run the portion of the General Meeting pertaining to the election of candidates,
   g. allow for at least 1 scrutineer per candidate to be present for counting of votes,
   h. present a report announcing all successful candidates following conclusion of voting.

22. Administrative Officer (Secretary/Treasurer)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Administrative Officer shall be responsible for:

   a. receiving and replying to all correspondence on behalf of the association;
   b. organizing meetings, agendas (in consultation with the Presidents), and minutes;
   c. keeping relevant association papers in order;
   d. coordinating elections;
   e. maintaining the membership list;
   f. keeping and maintaining all financial records;
   g. keeping the association informed of its financial position;
   h. carrying out financial transactions as directed by the committee;
   i. ensuring that records are up to date and in good order;
   j. always insisting on a receipt or docket to validate any expenditure;
   k. ensuring the association has at least two and not more than three signatories who are committee members;
   l. ensuring that association funds are not misused at any time;
   m. collecting mail on behalf of the association regularly;
   n. lodging a notice with the association specifying their address as soon as practicable after election.

23. Presidents
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Presidents shall be jointly responsible for:

   a. chairing all association, Committee, General and Annual General Meetings;
   b. overseeing and coordinating the activities and administration of the association;
   c. ensuring that the elected officers perform duties as laid down by the constitution;
   d. ensuring that all other tasks necessary for running the association are performed;
   e. having a thorough knowledge of the association's constitution;
   f. planning the coming year's activities;
   g. acting as official spokespersons for the association;
   h. arriving at a membership fee with the committee;
   i. liaising with fellow office bearers;
   j. ensuring that all required reaffiliation documentation is submitted within prescribed time periods;
   k. ensuring that the Administrative Officer submits Financial Reports;
   l. passing on their knowledge to their successors.

24. Vice-President (Internals) and Welfare Officer
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Vice-President (Internals) and Welfare Officer shall be responsible for:

   a. facilitating culture and team relations within the association;
   b. encouraging the software development culture at UNSW;
   c. facilitating opportunities for open-source development;
   d. receiving complaints and grievances relating to the association;
   e. investigating and resolving grievances or making recommendations to the committee;
   f. acting in a fair, ethical and confidential manner;
   g. fostering an inclusive culture within the association;
   h. facilitating and promoting the engagement of non-majority demographics;
   i. ensuring the association considers needs of non-majority demographics in its events;
   j. ensuring all association communications can be understood clearly by all students;
   k. being an accessible contact for matters regarding equitable events and diversity;
   l. providing guidance to representatives on inclusive communication and behavior;
   m. monitoring engagement and membership of students from non-majority demographics.

25. Vice-President (Project Operations)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Vice-President (Project Operations) shall be responsible for:

   a. supervising the management of the association's technical infrastructure;
   b. ensuring the visual cohesion of the association's technical projects;
   c. overseeing the development of association-internal technical projects;
   d. overseeing the long-term support of the association's technical projects;
   e. assisting the committee on their duties wherever practical.

26. Vice-President (Externals)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Vice-President (Externals) shall be responsible for:

   a. overseeing the association's social media presence and branding;
   b. overseeing the association's media and content creation processes;
   c. managing the association's external stakeholder relationships;
   d. overseeing the building of constructive partnerships with other societies, industry partners and the school;
   e. overseeing the association's undertaking of external facing events.

27. Vice-Presidents (Projects)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Vice-Presidents (Projects) shall be responsible for:

   a. managing existing project teams and timelines;
   b. receiving feedback from the student community and communicating needs to relevant projects;
   c. overseeing the development of software projects that cater to varying needs of the student body;
   d. assisting the committee on their duties wherever practical.

28. Delegation to Subcommittees
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) The committee may:

   a. establish one or more subcommittees to assist the committee, and
   b. appoint one or more members of the association to be members of the subcommittee.

(2) The committee may delegate to the subcommittee the exercise of functions specified, other than this power of delegation or a duty imposed by the Act.

Division 2 — Procedure
----------------------

29. Committee Meetings
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) The committee must meet at least 3 times in each 12-month period at the place and time determined by the committee.

(2) Additional meetings may be called by any committee member.

30. Notice of Committee Meeting
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) The Administrative Officer must give each committee member oral or written notice of a meeting at least 48 hours before the time the meeting is due to commence.

(2) The notice must describe the general nature of the business to be transacted.

(3) The only business that may be transacted at the meeting is the business described in the notice and business that committee members present unanimously agree is urgent business.

31. Quorum
~~~~~~~~~~

(1) The quorum for a meeting of the committee is 4 committee members.

(2) No business may be transacted unless a quorum is present.

(3) If a quorum is not present within half an hour of the time the meeting commences, the meeting is adjourned to the same time and place the following week.

32. Presiding Committee Member
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) The following committee member presides at a meeting:

   a. one of the Presidents, or
   b. if both Presidents are absent, one of the members present as elected by the other members.

(2) The member presiding has a deliberative vote and, in the event of an equality of votes, a second or casting vote.

33. Voting
~~~~~~~~~~

A decision supported by a majority of the votes cast at a meeting of the committee at which a quorum is present is the decision of the committee.

34. Acts Valid Despite Vacancies or Defects
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) The committee may act despite there being a casual vacancy.

(2) An act done by the committee is not invalidated because of a defect relating to the qualifications or appointment of a member.

35. Transaction of Business Outside Meetings
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) The committee may transact its business by circulation of papers, including by electronic means.

(2) A written resolution, approved in writing by a majority of committee members, is taken to be a decision of the committee made at a meeting.

(3) The committee may transact business at a meeting where one or more committee members participate by telephone or other electronic means, provided members can hear each other.

Part 4 — General Meetings of Association
========================================

36. Annual General Meetings
---------------------------

(1) The association must hold its first annual general meeting within 18 months of registration.

(2) Subsequent annual general meetings must be held within 6 months of the last day of the association's financial year.

(3) The business that may be transacted at an annual general meeting includes:

   a. confirming the minutes of previous meetings,
   b. receiving reports from the committee on the association's activities,
   c. electing office-bearers and committee members,
   d. receiving and considering financial statements or reports,
   e. constitutional amendments and other motions on notice.

(4) The Chair will hand over the meeting to the Returning Officer who will hold elections or announce winners and hand the meeting to the new Incoming President or a duly elected Chair.

(5) Full minutes of this meeting, including a list of the new committee, written financial reports, and constitutional amendments, shall be forwarded to Arc within fourteen (14) days of the meeting.

37. Special General Meetings (Extraordinary General Meetings)
-------------------------------------------------------------

(1) The committee may call a special general meeting whenever the committee thinks fit.

(2) The committee must call a special general meeting if the committee receives a written request from at least twenty (20) members or half of the association membership, whichever is the lesser.

(3) Such a petitioned meeting must be held within twenty-one (21) days, but no sooner than fourteen (14) days.

(4) The format, procedures, notice and quorum for a special general meeting shall be the same as for an Annual General Meeting, except that committee elections will not be held unless specifically notified.

38. Notice of General Meeting
-----------------------------

(1) The Administrative Officer must give each member notice of a general meeting:

   a. at least 21 days before the meeting if a matter requires a special resolution, or
   b. at least 14 days before the meeting otherwise.

(2) Notice may be given in writing to all members or displayed in a way that will guarantee an acceptable level of exposure among members.

(3) The notice must specify:

   a. the place and time at which the meeting will be held, and
   b. the nature of the business to be transacted, and
   c. if a special resolution is proposed, that fact, and
   d. for an annual general meeting, that the meeting is an annual general meeting.

(4) The only business that may be transacted is the business specified in the notice and, for annual general meetings, business referred to in clause 36(3).

39. Quorum
----------

(1) Quorum for an Annual or Special General Meeting shall be:

   a. Ten (10) or one half of the association membership, whichever is the lesser, for all associations with less than 75 members, and for any other association that has been active for less than 18 months; or
   b. Fifteen (15) ordinary members for all associations with more than 75 members that have been active for more than 18 months. An ordinary member is defined as a member who did not serve as committee member in the current year.

(2) No business may be transacted unless a quorum is present.

(3) If a quorum is not present within half an hour of the time the meeting commences, the meeting:

   a. if called on request of members, is dissolved, or
   b. otherwise, is adjourned to the same time and place the following week.

(4) If a quorum is not present within half an hour of the time an adjourned meeting commences, but there are at least 3 members present, the members present constitute a quorum.

40. Adjourned Meetings
----------------------

(1) The member presiding at a general meeting may, with consent of the majority of members present, adjourn the meeting to another time and place.

(2) Only the business remaining from the meeting at which the adjournment took place may be transacted at the adjourned meeting.

(3) If a meeting is adjourned for at least 14 days, the Administrative Officer must give each member notice, at least 1 day before the adjourned meeting, of the time, place and nature of business.

41. Presiding Member
--------------------

(1) The following member presides at a general meeting:

   a. one of the Presidents, or
   b. if both Presidents are absent, one of the members present as elected by the other members.

(2) The member presiding has a deliberative vote and, in the event of an equality of votes, a second or casting vote.

42. Voting
----------

(1) Voting at meetings shall be with a simple majority required for a resolution to be passed.

(2) Each full member is entitled to one vote.

(3) Directed proxies shall be allowed in meetings. Only a Returning Officer, or in their absence, meeting Chair, may hold proxy votes.

(4) A question raised at the meeting must be decided by:

   a. a show of hands, or
   b. a written ballot, if the member presiding moves or at least 5 members agree.

(5) In the case of equality of voting a countback will be held, with the candidate that received the most first preference votes winning. If there is another tie, the second preference votes will determine the winner, and so on.

(6) In the event a vote is completely and evenly matched, a re-vote will be held.

(7) Constitutional changes must be in the form of a motion on notice to an Annual or Extraordinary General Meeting.

(8) Constitutional changes passed at a meeting must be approved by Arc for the association to remain affiliated with Arc.

(9) A member cannot cast a vote by proxy.

43. Postal, Electronic or Combined Ballots
------------------------------------------

(1) The association may hold a postal, electronic or combined ballot as determined by the committee to decide any matter other than an appeal.

(2) The ballot must be conducted in accordance with Schedule 2 of the Regulation.

44. Transaction of Business Outside Meetings
--------------------------------------------

(1) The association may transact its business by circulation of papers, including by electronic means.

(2) A written resolution approved by a majority of members is taken to be a decision made at a general meeting.

(3) The association may transact business at a meeting where one or more members participate by telephone or other electronic means, provided members can hear each other.

=======================
Part 5 — Administration
=======================

45. Change of Name, Objects or Constitution
-------------------------------------------

An application for registration of a change in the association's name, objects or constitution must be made by the public officer or a committee member.

46. Funds
---------

(1) The association's funds may be derived from:

   a. entrance fees and annual subscription fees payable by members,
   b. donations,
   c. other sources as determined by the committee.

(2) The association's funds and assets must be used to pursue the association's objects in the way the committee determines.

(3) As soon as practicable after receiving money, the association must:

   a. deposit the money to the credit of the association's bank account, and
   b. issue a receipt.

(4) The association shall hold an account with a financial institution approved by Arc.

(5) The committee must approve all accounts and expenditures for payment.

(6) All financial transactions shall require two signatures of members of the committee.

(7) The association shall nominate three members of the committee as possible signatories for the account, one of which must be the Administrative Officer.

(8) The financial records of the association shall be open for inspection by Arc at all times.

47. Insurance
-------------

The association may take out and maintain insurance as appropriate for the association's assets and liabilities.

48. Non-Profit Status
---------------------

(1) The assets and income of the association shall be applied solely in furtherance of its objects and no portion shall be distributed directly or indirectly to the members except as bona fide compensation for services rendered or expenses incurred on behalf of the association.

(2) Subject to the Act and the Regulation, the association must not conduct its affairs in a way that provides a pecuniary gain for a member.

49. Service of Notices
----------------------

(1) A notice may be given to or served on a person:

   a. by delivering the notice to the person personally, or
   b. by sending the notice by pre-paid post to the address of the person, or
   c. by sending the notice by electronic transmission to an address specified by the person.

(2) A notice is taken to have been given or served:

   a. for a notice given personally, on the date received,
   b. for a notice sent by post, on the date it would have been delivered in ordinary course of post, or
   c. for a notice sent by electronic transmission, on the date sent.

50. Custody of Records and Books
--------------------------------

All records, books and other documents relating to the association must be kept in New South Wales at the association's main premises or official address, in the custody of the public officer or a member as determined by the committee.

51. Inspection of Records and Books
-----------------------------------

(1) The following documents must be available for inspection, free of charge, by members at a reasonable time:

   a. this constitution,
   b. minutes of committee meetings and general meetings,
   c. records, books and other documents relating to the association.

(2) A member may obtain a hard copy of a document on payment of a fee of not more than $1 per page.

(3) The committee may refuse to allow a member to inspect or obtain a copy of a document that relates to confidential, personal, commercial, employment or legal matters, or if the committee considers it would be prejudicial to the interests of the association.

52. Financial Year
------------------

The association's financial year is each period of 12 months commencing on 1 July and ending on the following 30 June.

Part 6 — Dissolution
====================

53. Dissolution of the Association
----------------------------------

(1) Dissolution of the association will occur after the following conditions have been met:

   a. An Extraordinary General Meeting is petitioned in writing by twenty (20) members or three-quarters of the association membership, whichever is the lesser;
   b. Procedures for notification are followed, and the reasons for the proposed dissolution are included with the notification to Arc;
   c. Quorum for the meeting to dissolve the association shall be twenty (20) members or three-quarters of the association membership, whichever is the lesser;
   d. No other business may be conducted at the meeting to dissolve the association;
   e. After the petitioning body has stated its case any opposition must be given the opportunity to reply, with at least ten minutes set aside for this purpose;
   f. A vote is taken and the motion to dissolve lapses if opposed by fifteen (15) or more members;
   g. If the motion to dissolve is carried, Arc must be notified within fourteen (14) days.

(2) Dissolution of the association will also occur if the association has been financially and administratively inactive for a period of eighteen (18) months.

54. Distribution of Property on Winding Up
------------------------------------------

(1) In a winding up of the association, the surplus property of the association must be transferred to another organisation:

   a. with similar objects, and
   b. which is charitable at law, and
